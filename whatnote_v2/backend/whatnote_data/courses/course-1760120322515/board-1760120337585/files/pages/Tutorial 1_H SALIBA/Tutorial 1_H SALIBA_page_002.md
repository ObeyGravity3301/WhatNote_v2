2- Pasteur’s Swan neck experiment  
 
It is very hard to challenge people’s believes! Despite Redi’s work, many scientists 
still believed in the theory of spontaneous generation until the middle of the 1800’s.   
 
Pasteur conducted the experiment of the “Swan neck flask” to further support Redi’s 
theory.  This experiment is illustrated in figure 2

Figure 2: The Swan neck experiment 
 
A) if a flask containing boiled clear broth is left open on a shelf, after several days, it 
becomes turbid because of the growth of bacteria 
 
B) - If the clear broth is introduced in a “swan neck container” with a curved 
opening, it can remain clear for a long period of time (months). However, if it is 
tipped as shown and then returned to a straight position and allowed to sit for 
several days, it will become turbid.  
 
Interpret this experiment and explain how it further confirmed Redi’s theory.
