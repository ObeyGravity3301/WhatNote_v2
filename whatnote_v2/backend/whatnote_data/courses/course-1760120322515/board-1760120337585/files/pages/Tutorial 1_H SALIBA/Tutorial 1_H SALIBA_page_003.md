3- Pasteur’s chicken cholera experiment  
 
Pasteur observed that chicken cholera could be transmitted through feces from one 
chicken group to another distant group.  He cultured the fecal extract (containing 
cholera bacterium) in broth, then performed the experiment illustrated in figure 2.  
 
He injected 3 different cholera bacterium cultures to chicken:  
- Group 1 :  a fresh culture is injected to healthy chicken 
- Group 2:  an aged culture (that was allowed to sit for several days on a shelf) is 
injected to healthy chicken  
- Group 3: After several days, some chicken from group 2 receive an injection of a 
fresh culture  
 
Questions:  
- What happens to the culture when it is aged for several days? 
- What happens to each group of chicken and why?  
- How do you interpret this experiment?  
- This experiment was a turning point in the history of microbiology and 
immunology and was further developed in a life-saving medical practice.  What is 
it?
