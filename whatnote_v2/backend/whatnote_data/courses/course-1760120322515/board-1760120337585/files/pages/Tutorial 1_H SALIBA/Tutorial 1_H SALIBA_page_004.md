4- Ivanovski’s experiment on the Tobacco Mosaic Virus (TMV ) 
 
Following the successful experiments of Louis Pasteur proving the bacterial origin of many 
transmissible diseases, including chicken cholera and rabies, one of his students, 
Chamberland, designed a porcelain filter with tiny pores that can retain bacteria. When 
bacteria-contaminated fluids are passed through this filter, they lose their infectivity.     
 
The Tobacco Mosaic disease attacks the leaves of the tobacco plant, inducing yellowing and
